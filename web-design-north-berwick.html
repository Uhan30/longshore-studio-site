Why North Berwick specifically

A seasonal town needs a site that works hardest when you're busiest.

North Berwick's trade leans hard into summer weekends, the Seabird Centre crowd, and a steady flow of Edinburgh day-trippers. A site built for that has to load fast on a phone held in one hand outside a shop, show real photography over stock images, and make booking or calling the obvious next step — not bury it three menus deep.

Common gap

No mobile booking path

Most passing trade is on a phone. If calling or booking takes more than one tap, it's a lost enquiry.

Common gap

Reviews not shown

A lot of North Berwick's strongest businesses have genuinely good Google reviews sitting completely unused on their own site.

Common gap

Stock photography

A generic beach photo doesn't sell your actual place. Real photography of the real business does.
