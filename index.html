Why

A website that only describes your business isn't finished.

Most small-business websites are a brochure: a few pages, a phone number, and then nothing happens until someone calls. Meanwhile the calls you miss, the questions you get asked on repeat, and the enquiries that go quiet overnight are the actual cost.

I build the site and the systems around it — so it answers questions itself, texts back a missed caller before they've hung up on someone else, and gets every enquiry in front of you the same day. Built by one person, in East Lothian, who answers the phone and holds the camera too.

You're up a ladder, mid-job, phone rings. The caller gets a text back in your name before they've had the chance to ring the next name on their list.
It's 9pm on a Sunday and someone's on your site. They ask about pricing, opening hours, whether you take dogs — and get a real answer, not silence until Monday.
You're with a customer and can't check your phone. The next enquiry doesn't wait for you to be free — it's logged, and you see it the moment you're clear.
You come home tired and don't want admin. One monthly report tells you exactly what got rescued and what needs a callback — nothing to dig for.
