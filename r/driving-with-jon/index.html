How it works

You keep working. The phone keeps working for you.

Most customers don't leave a voicemail. They ring the next name on the list. This closes that gap, whatever line of work you're in.

  1. Ring the demo line

    Hang up and watch your phone for the text. Or let it ring out and hear the voice that takes the job details. That is what your customers get when you can't answer.

  2. We set it up on your number

    You tell me your business name and the wording you'd like. I do the rest, usually the same day. No call needed.

  3. Missed calls become jobs

    You get their reply, or a text summary of the call, on your phone. Each week I send a short count of the calls caught, so you can see if it's paying for itself.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Is it only for trades?

No. Trades are who I built it for first, but it suits any owner-run business that misses calls while working with a customer: salons and barbers, physios and massage therapists, groomers, garages, driving instructors, cleaners, removals, trainers, wedding and event suppliers, B&Bs and small letting firms.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. Your number stays exactly as it is. If you don't answer, the call is passed to us and your caller gets a text from you, or, with call answering, a friendly voice takes their details.

What happens if someone rings and I don't answer?

With call answering, a friendly voice picks up, asks what they need and where, and takes their name and number. You get a text with a summary of the job. Ring the demo line and let it ring out to hear it.

What does the text say?

Something like "Sorry I missed your call, I'm on a job. What do you need and where?" You choose the wording, so a salon might say "I'm with a client" instead. I'll set it up. Ring the demo line to see one.

What's the catch with 7 days free?

There isn't one. If it isn't catching you work, tell me and it stops. Nothing to pay and no card needed to start.

Does it work on a landline?

Yes, as long as calls you miss can be passed on. I'll check when you sign up and tell you exactly what to do.

Can I cancel?

Any time, by email. No contract and no notice period.

Do you build websites too?

Yes, from £495. Drone footage, 360 tours and photography can be added if you need them.
