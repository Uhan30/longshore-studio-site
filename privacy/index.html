Who we are

Longshore Studio provides call answering and related tools for local UK businesses. For privacy questions, email hello@longshorestudio.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Website visitors: basic analytics if enabled (pages viewed, approximate location). We aim to use privacy-friendly settings.
  • Business prospects we email: business name, published email, town and trade from public websites, and whether you replied or opted out.
  • Callers to a client or demo line: phone number, a transcript of what you said, and any name or booking details you give, so we can text the business a summary.
  • Trial and paying clients: contact name, email, phone, business details, billing information, and service settings.

Why we use it

  • To run an automated call answering service and send text summaries for businesses that use us.
  • To reply to enquiries and run free trials.
  • To keep records we need for tax and the law.
  • To improve the service. We do not sell your personal data.

Lawful basis (UK GDPR)

  • Contract — providing the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — contacting a business about a relevant service, and keeping a suppression list so we honour opt-outs.
  • Consent — where you opt in, or where the law needs consent.
  • Legal obligation — tax and accounting records.

Call handling

Calls to our demo line and to client lines that use our answering service are transcribed so we can text the business a short summary. A short notice is given at the start of the call. Transcripts are kept only as long as needed (see below).

Who we share with

We use service providers under contract to help us run the service — for example telephony and SMS providers, and hosting providers. They only process data for us under those contracts. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where the law allows, or ask us to stop marketing to you. Email hello@longshorestudio.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Cookies

If we use non-essential cookies or similar tools, we will describe them here and, where required, ask before they run. Essential cookies needed for the site to work may be used without a prompt.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
